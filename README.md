# One engineer. An autonomous company of 130+ AI employees.

Everything on this profile was built by one engineer, Tanuj Jain, directing an
AI workforce: more than 100 AI employees across 22 departments, working around
the clock under a written charter.

The code is private. The activity is public on purpose.

## What the numbers mean

- **5,000+ contributions since September 2026**, across 15 private repositories
- **1,000+ merged pull requests** in that time (Pull Shark, top tier)
- **Co-authored commits throughout** (Pair Extraordinaire, top tier): each AI
  employee is credited on the work it did

## How changes are reviewed

Every change is produced inside the FaultScope Autonomous Company. A change is
proposed, built, checked, and reviewed by AI employees in their own roles
before it becomes a pull request. The pull request is the last recorded step
before merge. No change reaches the main branch from outside that process.

GitHub's YOLO badge appears here because those reviews happen inside the
autonomous pipeline, not in GitHub's own review feature.

## What is being built

**FaultScope** applies the laws of physics to organizations: load, resistance,
yield, and accumulated strain. It reads the operational signals a company
already produces and shows where the organization will fail, more than 14 days
before it does.

## Why this profile exists

To show what one person can ship when the engineering organization itself is
autonomous: the volume, the pace, and the discipline behind it.
